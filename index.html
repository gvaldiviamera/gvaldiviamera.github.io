Gustavo Valdivia-Mera

I am a Ph.D. student in Physics at the University of Houston, currently working as a Research Assistant in the Hosur Group, based in the Department of Physics and the Texas Center for Superconductivity.

My current research focuses on theoretical condensed matter physics, particularly quantum many-body theory, topological quantum matter, and anomalous transport phenomena. I am also interested in quantum field theory in causally restricted domains, with emphasis on the emergence of thermality, information-theoretic structures, and their connections to quantum gravity.
